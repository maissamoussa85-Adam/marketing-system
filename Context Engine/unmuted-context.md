## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Who buys from us:**
- CMOs, Heads of Marketing, VPs of GTM, Founders
- High-ACV B2B SaaS companies with 1M+ ARR or early-stage funding
- Companies fighting for attention in saturated categories
- Clients include: Backbase, aNewSpring, Studytube, Neople, Aiden, Novulo, Sendcloud

**What they're struggling with:**
1. Market saturation -- "Every company is fighting for attention"
2. Feature commoditization -- AI-assisted development makes unique features easily replicable, eroding differentiation
3. Messaging gaps -- audiences don't "actually care" about the product because positioning is weak
4. Marketing drift -- "As reality changes rapidly, your marketing assets are probably drifting into irrelevance"
5. One-off campaigns that generate short-term noise but no lasting pipeline

**Why they start looking for a solution:**
- Hit a growth plateau despite having a good product
- Marketing spend isn't converting -- awareness without demand
- Realized positioning and storytelling need a serious overhaul
- Need senior expertise with hands-on execution, not just strategy decks

## Tone of Voice

**Style:**
- Direct and opinionated -- says what others in the industry won't
- Conversational, not corporate -- talks like a sharp colleague, not a consultant
- Results-focused -- ties everything back to pipeline and ARR
- Confident without arrogance -- "Turns out this stuff works"
- No egos (literally one of their cultural values)

**Write like this:**
- "Turning B2B SaaS growth plateaus into ARR milestones."
- "Well, well, well. Would you look at that."
- "The thing is: good B2B SaaS marketing requires a long-term investment."
- "Not investing in video yet? You're setting your business up for failure."
- "Good marketing doesn't drive leads, it changes perceptions."

**Never write like this:**
- Vague promises without proof ("We help you grow your business")
- Corporate jargon and buzzword soup
- Passive voice
- Hedging or wishy-washy language
- One-off campaign thinking -- Unmuted is about sustained impact

## Key Messaging Angles

1. **The market is noisier than ever -- positioning is now a survival skill, not a nice-to-have.** AI-assisted development has commoditized features. The only real moat left is how you tell your story.
2. **B2B SaaS marketing is a marathon, not a sprint.** One-off projects deliver suboptimal results. Real demand takes time, consistency, and a long-term GTM engine.
3. **Most B2B marketing doesn't change perceptions -- it just makes noise.** Unmuted builds campaigns that shift how buyers think, not just campaigns that show up in their feed.
