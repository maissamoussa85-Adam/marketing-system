Embedding AI into Real Workflows (Q2 2026)

THE WHY (The Problem)

AI Document Corruption is Real.

  • Recent independent studies (DELEGATE-52 benchmark) prove even frontier LLMs corrupt 25% of document content during automated workflows.
  • Enterprise PMs and Engineering Leads are hesitant to embed AI because it breaks rich-text formatting (tables, lists, headers).
  • The insight: The solution isn't a better LLM; it's a better editing interface with human-in-the-loop governance.

THE HOW (The Execution)

Shift Cold Accounts to Warm.

  • The Offer: "5% AI Ebook - Embedding AI into Real Workflows". High-value content to capture intent without the friction of a "Free Trial".
  • Distribution: A 5-ad LinkedIn rotation (1:1 square) targeting Product Managers, Lead SWEs, and UX Specialists.
  • Messaging Pillars: Governance, Security, Speed, AI Tooling, and Simplicity.
  • Next Step: Nurture eBook downloaders into sales-assisted conversations.
